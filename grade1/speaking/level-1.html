Level 1

👋 Introduce Yourself

Say your name and answer simple questions.

Today's Goal

By the end of this practice, you can introduce yourself using simple English sentences.

Let's Practice

Question 1

What is your name?

Em tên là gì?

Example answer
My name is Anna.
🗣️ Say your own name instead of “Anna”.
Question 2

How are you today?

Hôm nay em khỏe không?

Example answer
I'm good.
🗣️ You can also say: “I'm happy.” or “I'm great.”
Question 3

How old are you?

Em bao nhiêu tuổi?

Example answer
I'm seven years old.
🗣️ Change the number to your age.

🎯 Speaking Challenge

Can you introduce yourself?

Try to answer all three questions without looking at the example answers.

My name is...
I'm ...
