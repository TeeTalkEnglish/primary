Level 2

How Are You?

Talk about how you feel using simple English.

Today's Goal

By the end of this practice, you can talk about how you feel and answer simple questions about your feelings using English sentences.

Let's Practice

Question 1

How are you?

Bạn khỏe không? / Bạn cảm thấy thế nào?

Example answer
I'm happy.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • I'm good.
  • I'm great.
  • I'm fine.
Question 2

Are you happy today?

Hôm nay bạn có vui không?

Example answer
Yes, I am.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • Yes, I'm happy.
  • No, I'm not.
Question 3

Are you sad?

Bạn có buồn không?

Example answer
No, I'm not.
I'm happy.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • Yes, I am.
  • No, I'm not.
  • I'm a little sad.
Question 4

Are you tired?

Bạn có mệt không?

Example answer
Yes, I am.
I'm tired.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • No, I'm not.
  • I'm a little tired.
  • I'm very tired.
Question 5

Are you hungry?

Bạn có đói không?

Example answer
Yes, I am.
I'm hungry.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • No, I'm not.
  • I'm a little hungry.
  • I'm very hungry.
Question 6

How do you feel today?

Hôm nay bạn cảm thấy thế nào?

Example answer
I feel great today.
🗣️ You can also say:
  • I feel happy.
  • I feel good.
  • I feel tired.
  • I feel excited.
