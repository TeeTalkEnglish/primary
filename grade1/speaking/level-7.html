Level 7

👋 Saying Goodbye

Learn simple, polite ways to say goodbye.

Today's Goal

By the end of this practice, you can use simple English expressions to end a conversation politely.

Let's Learn

Expression 1

Goodbye!

Tạm biệt!

Example
Goodbye, Mom!
Expression 2

See you later!

Hẹn gặp lại!

Example
See you later, Ben!
Expression 3

See you next time!

Hẹn gặp lại lần sau!

Example
See you next time, teacher!

Speaking Practice

Sample conversation

Teacher: It's time to go. Goodbye!

Student: Goodbye, teacher!

Teacher: See you next time!

Student: See you later!

Say it aloud

Practice each expression. Say each one aloud three times.

  1. Goodbye!
  2. See you later!
  3. See you next time!

Now take turns with a friend. One person says goodbye, and the other person answers.
