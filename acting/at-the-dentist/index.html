TEETALK ENGLISH • GRADE 3

AT THE DENTIST

FADE IN:
INT. DENTIST'S OFFICE – DAY

A CHILD sits in the dentist's chair. The child holds their cheek and looks worried.

DENTIST

(smiling)

Hello. What's wrong?

PATIENT

(holding their cheek)

I have a toothache.

The dentist moves closer and looks carefully at the patient's teeth.

DENTIST

Let me check.

PATIENT

Okay.

DENTIST

Open your mouth, please.

The patient opens their mouth.

PATIENT

Ahhh!

The dentist carefully checks the tooth.

DENTIST

(calmly)

Don't worry.

PATIENT

(worried)

Is it bad?

DENTIST

(smiling)

No. But you need to take good care of your teeth.

The patient smiles and nods.

PATIENT

Okay. Thank you, Doctor.

DENTIST

You're welcome.

FADE OUT.

Step 4

🎭 Acting Section

Don't just say the words. Act the character!

😣

Patient

Show the dentist that your tooth hurts.

  • Hold your cheek.
  • Look worried.
  • Look nervous.
  • Open your mouth.
  • Look relieved at the end.
🦷

Dentist

Be a friendly and calm dentist.

  • Smile and say hello.
  • Look carefully at the tooth.
  • Speak calmly.
  • Help the patient feel safe.
  • Give friendly advice.

Practice

🗣️ Practice Your Lines

Take 1: Read your lines slowly.

Take 2: Use emotion in your voice.

Take 3: Add facial expressions and body language.

Take 4: Try the scene without reading.

Final Challenge

🎬 Lights! Camera! Action!

Perform the whole scene with your partner.

Use your English, your voice, your face, and your body.

Be the character.

Be the movie star!
